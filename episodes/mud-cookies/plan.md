# What if you only ate mud cookies?

**Original production reference:** [Lava pilot](../lava-pool/exports/lava-pool-character-revision.mp4)  
**Status:** Verified full export  
**Premise:** A fictional adult drinks water as usual and replaces every meal with clay-heavy cookies that provide very little energy or protein.  
**Target runtime:** 66 seconds  
**Opening hook:** “What if you only ate mud cookies?”  
**Main event on screen and when it happens:** The lead bites into the first cookie at 0:02.4. The cookie cracks, grit sticks to the teeth, and the jaw stops halfway through the chew.  
**Ending payoff:** Match the opening plate angle. The character reaches past the cookies for the phone. The untouched stack fills the foreground as the narrator answers the premise. The story ends on Day 7.  
**Narrator and tone:** Approved lava-pilot Chatterbox Nano CPU narrator with the same local reference WAV and half-semitone lift. Start matter-of-fact, pause for the first crunch, speed up lightly during the bathroom beat, and go quiet after the final gag.

## Story and sources

Day 1, Day 3, Day 5, and Day 7 are timestamps in one fictional story. Use a small “Dramatized timeline” line under the first day marker. Show drinking water in the kitchen setups. Each internal clip starts from a visible action, lasts roughly three to four seconds, and returns to the character mid-action. Use the same textures and contrast inside and outside the body.

- On Day 1, the stomach mixes the swallowed material with digestive juices. The cutaway shows grainy particles churning through liquid. [NIDDK digestive system guide](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/digestive-system-how-it-works)
- Clay eating has been reported alongside abdominal pain and constipation. As the large intestine absorbs water, stool can get dry and hard when it moves through slowly. Show the hard stool forming in the colon, then return to the same straining posture before the bathroom payoff. [Published clay ingestion case](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8118014/) · [NIDDK constipation symptoms](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/constipation/symptoms-causes)
- Day 5 shows the difference between food present in the gut and nutrients absorbed from it. The small intestine normally absorbs nutrients from digested food. In this fictional diet, the cookies contain very little usable energy or protein. The few particles crossing the intestinal lining represent the diet's low nutrient content. [NIDDK digestive system guide](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/digestive-system-how-it-works)
- Under a large energy shortfall, the body draws on its reserves, including stored fat. Show that process in a tissue inset as the character tries to stand. The effort and cramp depict the fictional character’s combined discomfort and exhaustion. The ending stays with these effects through Day 7. [Seven-day fasting study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-55418-0)

## Character contacts

- **Day 1.** Both feet hold under the chair. The cookie hand reaches first. At 0:02.42 the jaw and head recoil before the hips settle. The hand drops after contact, then reaches for the water after the gag.
- **Day 3.** The cookie hand stops mid-bite and the far arm draws toward the abdomen. In the bathroom, the hips stay over the toilet seat, heels stay planted, and the hands brace on the thighs. The shoulders lean forward in short pushes and ease back after the drop.
- **Day 5.** Both feet stay planted. The near hand brings the glass up before the intestine cutaway and keeps it there on the return. It trembles before the wrist tips the rim against the lips and lowers it to the table.
- **Day 7.** Both feet move into position before the rise. The near palm braces from about 0:46 through the fall back into the chair at 0:54. The hips rise through the tissue cutaway, the knees soften, and the torso folds forward again for the retch. The phone slides toward the plate before the final close-up.
