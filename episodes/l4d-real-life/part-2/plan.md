## Core Part 2 Workflow & Pacing Rules (Upgraded from Part 1 Lessons)

1. **ZERO Long Plain Shots ("Interactive Multi-Angle Pacing Rule"):**
   - **Never hold one plain single-angle action for 5–7+ seconds** (e.g., never show 7 seconds of just side-view running or just plain walking).
   - **Target `1.5–4.0 s` per camera setup** (`23–26` distinct shots per ~80 s chapter!).
   - Whenever an action spans more than ~3.5 seconds (climbing a ladder, searching alleys, fighting the Hunter, sprinting to cover, looting the workshop), **break it into interactive angle shifts**: `wide/side body action (2.0 s)` → `tactile foot/hand/prop close-up (1.5 s)` → `front/eye-dart reaction (1.8 s)` → `subjective POV rush or sweep (2.0 s)` → `OTS or glance-back threat check (1.6 s)`.
2. **100% Native 24 fps Smooth OpenToonz Animation:**
   - Always pass continuous floating-point `local` frames to `rigAt(scene, local)` — never `Math.round()`, so every climb, lunge, slash, sprint, and shove stays crisp and fluid.
   - All forearm wraps, gloves, cuffs, and held props render inside unified bone transforms (zero arm/cuff misalignment).
3. **Zero Three-Quarter Turnarounds:**
   - Strictly Front, Side, and Back turnaround views + `drawCinematicAngle` (`ots`, `overhead`, `low-angle`, `high-angle`) + `drawFaceAngle` (`eye-dart`, `head-tilt`, `look-up`, `look-down`, `glance-back`, `ecu-dread`) + studio POV hands (`pov-door-lock`, `pov-pipe-grip`, `pov-flashlight`, `pov-shield-face`, `bar-hold`, `bar-press`).
4. **Conversational, Engaging Human Narration (Zero Robotic Fragments):**
   - Same approved **Dark Horror Series Narrator** (`$0` local Chatterbox Nano CPU, approved reference WAV, `0.93–1.02×` speed, `<= 18` words/chunk, zero hyphens).
   - Talkative, vivid, grounded second-person storytelling that explains what you're seeing, feeling, and deciding in the moment—with deliberate `0.5–0.9 s` silent holds for key horror sound cues (iron ladder clank, phone buzz, Hunter claw scrape, blade snap, shotgun rack).
