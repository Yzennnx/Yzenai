## Core Part 3 Workflow, Pacing & Quality Guardrails

1. **Interactive Multi-Angle Pacing (`24–28` shots per chapter, `2.0–3.8 s` avg):** Never hold a single plain side-view walk, run, or stairwell climb for 5–7+ seconds. Split sustained motion into rapid interactive setups (`side action` → `boot/hand/prop close-up` → `front reaction / glance-back` → `subjective POV` → `OTS threat check`).
2. **Strict Continuity & Exact Ammo/Equipment Tracking:** Every shell fired, every pistol round spent, every tear in the clothing, and every wound coordinate carries unbroken from shot to shot and chapter to chapter.
3. **Zero Three-Quarter Turnarounds & Unified Bone Transforms:** Strictly Front, Side, and Back turnaround views + `drawCinematicAngle` (`ots`, `overhead`, `low-angle`, `high-angle`) + `drawFaceAngle` + studio POV hands. Render leather gloves, torn cardboard wraps, cream cuffs, and weapons inside **one unified coordinate transform** so seams never drift; keep backpack edges behind the torso in Front view.
4. **Articulated Creature Anatomy & Zero Severed Limbs:** Witch, Boomer, Spitter, Charger, and Tank all use curved, jointed limb anatomy (head/neck, hunched spine/ribs, elbows/knees, hands/claws)—never flat triangles or stick rectangles. Every close-up boot, leg, or arm extends cleanly past the frame edge.
5. **Ground vs. Standing State Continuity:** When the lead dives behind the bollard (`Ch 3C`), dives from the marble slab (`Ch 3D`), or drops to his knees on the helipad (`Ch 3F`), keep him physically grounded until an explicit scramble-up or rescue lift.
6. **Time Realism & Native 24 fps Physics:** Bridge the midnight-to-pre-dawn crawl cleanly at the start of `Ch 3B` (`01:45 AM → 03:30 AM → 04:35 AM`) so there are zero unexplained multi-hour jumps. Pass continuous floating-point `local` frames to `rigAt(scene, local)`—never `Math.round()`.
7. **Pre-Render Visual Still QA & Parallel Subagent Safety:** Inspect key-pose stills across EVERY camera mode before committing to full renders. Lock each chapter's shot-timing contract before fanning out parallel `Audio/Voice`, `Poses/Render`, and `Scene/Visuals` work while keeping heavy CPU renders serialized.
