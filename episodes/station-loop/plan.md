# What if every exit sent you back to the same bench?

**Creative reference** — [Nightmare Roulette source study](../../references/long-form-camera-voice/source-study.md) and [camera, pacing, and voice guide](../../references/long-form-camera-voice/README.md).

**Status** — Opening motion test ready for review. The station loop was selected from the project handoff.

**Premise** — In an empty station, every marked exit returns you to the opposite door of the same waiting room.

**Format and aspect ratio** — 9:16 short. Target 52 seconds. Opening test 12 seconds at 24 fps.

**Opening hook** — “What if every exit led back to this bench?”

**Main event** — At 5.25 seconds the lead passes through right-hand door A and immediately comes through left-hand door B, still moving screen right.

**Ending payoff** — A service shutter rises. The lead picks up the ticket and crosses through. The bench recedes behind them through the open shutter.

**Narrator and tone** — Approved local Chatterbox Nano voice at +0.5 semitone. Curious question, short gap over the door cut, then a dry, concrete explanation.

## Story and sources

The room and its props persist. Crossing any marked exit moves the lead to the opposite door, preserving their forward direction and speed. An unmarked service shutter is outside that rule. The first return interrupts an ordinary walk. The running attempt carries full speed into the room, so the lead has to catch the bench. That contact leaves the paper ticket where it was. When the shutter sounds, the lead lifts their palm from the bench, retrieves the ticket, and tries the new route.

The [station blocking sketch](#station-blocking) fixes the screen geography. The point-of-view hand insert, sound-led return, and prop callback use the creative guide's staging and edit techniques.

### Station blocking

```text
BACK WALL     [B / EXIT]     [service shutter]     [A / EXIT]
FLOOR         return →        BENCH • ticket       → first exit
CAMERA        wide from platform side; POV along A's push bar
```

### Mark, run and brace

- **Viewer takeaway** — The loop preserves speed, forcing a stop against the bench.
- **Timing** — 12–33 s at 24 fps. Mark contact at 16 s; return on a run landing at 22 s; lead heel brakes at 26 s; palm meets wood at 26.25 s and holds until the shutter cue.
- **Staging and reference** — [Side run](../../assets/series-lead/anatomy-prototype/run-side.mp4), frames 0/9 landings and 8/17 flight. [Side brace](../../assets/series-lead/anatomy-prototype/brace.mp4), frames 25–52 for palm and front shoe hold. Reverse axis only after wide shows the entry into B.
- **Contact and continuity** — Near hand presses chalk against B and releases before the run. On return the leading shoe brakes first, pelvis advances, then near palm catches the wood and the free elbow follows behind. Ticket stays put.
- **Pass on mute** — Chalk touches the door, run carries through the cut and heel/palm stop is readable. Retry if the hand floats or the lead appears to stop before contact.
- **Preview and result** — Planned for the full short.

### Ticket pickup and shutter crossing

- **Viewer takeaway** — Lead takes the ticket and leaves the room through the open shutter.
- **Timing** — 33–52 s at 24 fps. Palm releases on the rattle, eyes lead the turn, ticket grip at 43 s, heel crosses sill at 48 s.
- **Staging and reference** — [Turnaround](../../assets/series-lead/reference-sheet.png) grip and rear three-quarter silhouette, [rear walk](../../assets/series-lead/anatomy-prototype/walk-rear.mp4) frames 0/14 for alternating heel contact. Ticket close-up uses the near hand again. The [hand study notes](../../assets/series-lead/README.md#close-up-hand-references) record sources for the original chalk and push-bar drawings.
- **Contact and continuity** — Weight settles onto near shoe as palm releases. Use the [three-quarter ticket grip](../../assets/series-lead/hands/ticket-pinch.png). The thumb and bent index meet on opposite faces at one corner before the ticket follows the wrist. Ticket stays in that hand through the sill crossing.
- **Pass on mute** — Bench seat visibly empties and the lead leaves through a different opening. Retry if the ticket moves before the pinch or the shutter occludes crossing.
- **Preview and result** — Planned for the full short.
