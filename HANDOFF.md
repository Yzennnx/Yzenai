# Next episode motion test

The station loop was selected for the first opening motion test. The [episode plan](episodes/station-loop/plan.md) tracks the current build and review. The three original brainstorm options are below.

## Premise options

| Premise | Opening and impossible event | Consequence and payoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What if every exit sent you back to the same bench?** | In an empty station, the lead leaves a ticket on a bench and walks through the exit. A first-person view of the push bar cuts to the lead stepping out of the opposite door, facing the same bench and ticket. | The lead marks the next door and runs through it. They pass the mark again and brace against the bench to stop. The ticket stays where it was left, and the bench becomes the one reliable landmark. A shutter outside the loop starts to rise, revealing a way out. |
| **What if the floor started moving like a treadmill?** | At a closed mall, a painted floor arrow slides under one shoe and the lead is carried away from the exit. | A faster run barely holds position. The lead catches a fixed rail and steps onto a stationary curb. A loose coin from the opening rolls by on the moving floor. |
| **What if you heard every footstep one second early?** | In a quiet stairwell, three footfalls sound while the lead is standing still. One second later, their shoes hit the steps in exactly that rhythm. | A sharp skid sounds before the lead reaches a wet landing, giving them just enough time to grab the rail and stop. End with a second set of footfalls while both of the lead's feet are visibly planted. |

For the station loop, start with one set and keep the bench, ticket, exit sign, and screen direction clear across cuts. The first pass shows the loop at walking speed. Save the running attempt for escalation. The premise is fictional, so record the portal rule in the plan.

## First test after a premise is chosen

1. Save the premise and opening shot list in an episode plan. Use a short test for the first pass and keep the option to expand the approved premise into a 3–5-minute episode.
2. Mock up the first-person hand-to-push-bar contact and the third-person reveal at the same bench. Include the first visible consequence in the opening preview. Audition two hook reads with the same narrator.
3. Verify the opening at phone size on mute, then check the cut and voice on playback. For the run and stop, check the same supporting foot and gait phase across angles, then check the planted foot and palm through contact and release. Record the first failing frame and rerender that range if needed.
